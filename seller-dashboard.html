CASSANO MARKETPLACE SELLER AGREEMENT

This Seller Agreement sets out the general rules and responsibilities applicable to sellers using Cassano Marketplace.

1. Seller Eligibility & Verification

Sellers must provide accurate registration information and may be required to provide identification or business information for verification.

2. Seller Account Responsibilities

The seller is responsible for keeping account information, login credentials and payment information secure and accurate.

3. Product Listing Rules

Product names, descriptions, prices, stock quantities, images, colors, sizes and other listing information must be accurate.

4. Authenticity & Prohibited Products

Sellers must not knowingly list counterfeit, stolen, illegal, unsafe or otherwise prohibited goods.

5. Pricing & Promotions

Sellers control their product prices and promotions but must ensure that displayed discounts and original prices are truthful.

6. Orders & Fulfillment

Sellers are responsible for processing accepted orders, maintaining sufficient stock and providing accurate order status.

7. Delivery Responsibilities

Sellers must provide accurate delivery information and cooperate with the applicable delivery process.

8. Returns, Refunds & Disputes

Sellers must cooperate with valid customer complaints, returns, refunds and dispute-resolution procedures applicable to the order.

9. Cassano Platform Commission

Cassano Marketplace's current standard platform commission is 10% of the applicable customer transaction value.

Example: if a customer pays TZS 100,000 for an applicable sale, a 10% platform commission is TZS 10,000 and the seller proceeds are TZS 90,000 before any other applicable costs or adjustments.

10. Payments & Withdrawals

Seller earnings may become available for withdrawal according to Cassano's applicable payment and settlement process. Actual payment processing requires secure backend/payment-provider integration.

11. Customer Data & Privacy

Sellers must use customer information only for legitimate marketplace transactions and must respect applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

12. Intellectual Property

Sellers must have the necessary rights to use product images, descriptions, logos and other content uploaded to the platform.

13. Fraud & Misrepresentation

Fraudulent transactions, fake products, misleading information, manipulation of orders or other dishonest activity may result in account restrictions or termination.

14. Account Suspension / Termination

Cassano may restrict or suspend seller access where there is evidence of serious violations, fraud, unsafe activity or repeated breaches of marketplace rules.

15. Complaints & Dispute Resolution

Sellers agree to cooperate with Cassano's applicable process for investigating customer complaints and resolving disputes.

16. Agreement Changes

Cassano may update seller rules, commission structures or other terms when necessary. Material changes should be communicated through appropriate platform channels.

17. Seller Acceptance

By accepting this agreement, the seller confirms that the information supplied to Cassano is accurate and agrees to comply with the marketplace seller rules.
